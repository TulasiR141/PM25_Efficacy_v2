# MAI Project Module

## Description
This repo describes the common expectations for projects under the supervision of Magda Gregorova.

## Usage
You can clone this repo and use it as a basis for your project. All artifacts related to the project shall be stored within this repo. This includes all requested deliverables as well as all working material.

The repo proposes a basic folder structure: 
* `code` - all project related software and documentation, e.g. readme with dependencies/requirements and user instructions
* `demo` - all material for project demo, see below for large files
* `paper` - all material for project final paper, this shall include the originals such as tex files and images as well as the final paper pdf
* `poster` - all material for project poster, this shall include the originals such as tex files and images as well as the final poster pdf
* `workings` - all working material for the project
  * `minutes` - for keeping meeting minutes
  * `proposal` - for initial project proposal

You can extend the folder structure as you see fit.

Large files such as data, videos etc. shall not be stored within the repo. Ask Magda to create a folder for you on the `fiw.cloud` for these.

## Project organization

### Rule #1: it is your project!
You shall assume the active role in all parts of the project organization and development. This includes but is not limited to all parts of project planning, definition of concrete project outcomes, strategy for development, team organization, meeting organization, project documentation and communication with external parties. 

My role as a supervisor is to assist you in this process. I can advise you on all the above but the final decisions as well as their execution is your responsibility. Of course within the limits of the PM rules and good academic conduct.

### Working time
The project module is worth 10 ECTS which corresponds to 300 study hours. This is equivalent to 20 hours per week over the span of the 15-week semester so about 50% standard working time in a regular employment. With 3 people in a team this is a lot and hence, the expectations on the project outcomes are correspondingly high.

The distribution of the working hours over the semester can be adapted according to the overall project planning and your personal needs. For example, you may decrease the efforts when preparing for exams or when waiting for inputs from external parties, you may need to increase the effort when preparing final project deliverables. It is, however, strongly recommended that you work on the project continuously throughout the semester. Remember that the project is equivalent to a bit over 7 weeks full-time job!

### Team collaboration
The projects are developed in teams of 3 students. You shall think about the key roles and responsibilities of each member so that they can best contribute to the team effort. Consider the skills and interests of each member and try to benefit from these. Try to balance the expected workload and encourage collaborative and knowledge-sharing environment. Though it is good to make each member accountable for their deliverables, remain open-minded and flexible. Roles in the project may need to be adapted as the project develops.  

Though the evaluation (grades) are individual, team work is an important part of the project module and enters the evaluation as a critical factor.

### Communication
All communication between the teams and myself shall always include all team members. Please refrain from individual one-to-one communication unless related to some private personal matters.

There are three basic types of communication between the teams and myself with differing structure and flexibility:

* meetings: typically organized by you. You shall propose a meeting date using the email scheduling tool to find a free slot, block it in our calendars, specify the meeting objectives/goals and prepare an agenda. 
  * The planning and scheduling of the meetings is your responsibility. My recommendation is regular bi-weekly updates subject to my availability limited by the need to attend to some other important matters or traveling.  
  * You shall prepare the supporting material for the meeting and talk me through it during the meeting. In case of questions, think them through before the meeting so we can answer them quickly and efficiently.
  * Meetings shall not extend 1 hour. Please prepare carefully to avoid delays caused by you improvising or searching for the background material.
  * Meetings shall be recorded within meeting minutes for future reference - see above the folder structure. 
  * I have a strong preference for physical meetings, generally in CAIRO. 
* [email](mailto:magda.gregorova@thws.de): for more complex questions, communication. I tend to respond within the same day.
* [mattermost](https://mattermost.fiw.fhws.de/): quick messaging, I tend to respond within a couple of minutes.
  
### Project planning and management
You can use any project planning and management tools you consider useful. Remember that there are some strict deadlines - posters, papers, demos - that you need to integrate into your planning. Having a clear timeline with internal deadlines and milestones is therefore highly recommendable. Try to think about all things that need to be done straight from the beginning, list them as tasks with estimated time / work effort the best you can and assign a responsible task-leader to each of these.

The work plan and timelines will need to be adapted as the project develops so be prepared to be flexible. I would discourage you from using Scrum and rather recommend some hybrid lightweight solution such as Kanban where you update the tasks as you identify the project needs. My primary recommendation is to keep the project management efficient and simple. 

### Working place and internal collaboration
The project module has no slot in the semester schedule. Hence, you can decide freely when and where you wish to work on it and where and how you wish to meet.

These are some possible options:

* CAIRO ground floor student room & Expo room - available freely on first-come-first-serve basis during the usual extended CAIRO working hours (8am-10pm, Mon-Sat)
* CAIRO meeting rooms - for group meetings, brainstorming sessions, etc., available upon agreement with me based on prior room booking up to several hours
* CAIRO offices - available for regular work during usual working hours (8:30am-6pm Mon-Fri). Advantages: common working space encouraging collaboration, some hardware support (larger screens), access to me or my PhDs for quick consultations. Disadvantages: limited capacity, only accessible with my PhDs or myself in the building.
